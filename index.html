How to Type Ñ (Enye) & Spanish Keyboard Shortcuts

If you are trying to type the Spanish Ntilde (Ñ/ñ) or accented letters (á, é, í, ó, ú) on a standard US keyboard, Chromebook, or Mac, here is the complete keyboard shortcut guide.

Quick Keyboard Shortcuts (ALT Codes)

Hold down the ALT key and type the following numbers on your numeric keypad:

  • Capital Ñ: ALT + 165 or ALT + 0209
  • Small ñ: ALT + 164 or ALT + 0241
  • Á (Capital A accent): ALT + 0193
  • á (Small a accent): ALT + 160 or ALT + 0225

How to Type Ñ on Mobile & Chromebook

On mobile keyboards (Android/iOS), long-press the letter 'N' or 'n' until the Ñ/ñ pop-up appears. On Chromebooks, press Ctrl + Shift + U, then type 00F1 for small ñ or 00D1 for capital Ñ.
