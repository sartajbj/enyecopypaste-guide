2. How to Type Ñ on macOS (Mac Keyboards)

Apple macOS makes typing accented characters simple through built-in keyboard shortcuts:

  • Option Key Method (Fastest): Press Option + N, release both keys, and then press N to produce lower-case ñ. To type capital Ñ, press Option + N, release, then press Shift + N.
  • Accent Menu Method: Press and hold the N key until a small popup menu displaying numbered variations appears. Press 1 or click the ñ character.

3. How to Type Ñ on Chromebooks (ChromeOS)

Chromebooks do not have a traditional numeric keypad, but you can use Unicode hex input or switch your keyboard layout:

  • Unicode Shortcut: Press Ctrl + Shift + U, release, type 00F1, and press Enter for small ñ. For capital Ñ, type 00D1.
  • US International Keyboard: Go to Chromebook Settings > Device > Keyboard > Change Input Settings. Add US International Keyboard. Once enabled, press AltGr (Right Alt) + N to type ñ instantly.

4. Typing Spanish Accents on Mobile (Android & iOS)

On smartphones and tablets, typing special characters is straightforward across touch keyboards (Gboard, Apple Keyboard, SwiftKey):

  1. Tap and hold the N key until extended character options pop up.
  2. Slide your finger to select ñ or Ñ and release.
  3. For accented vowels (á, é, í, ó, ú), press and hold the respective letter key (A, E, I, O, U) and choose the required accent.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Why is my ALT code not working on Windows?

Make sure your Num Lock key is turned ON and you are using the dedicated physical numeric keypad on the right side of your keyboard. Top-row number keys usually do not trigger ALT code inputs.
